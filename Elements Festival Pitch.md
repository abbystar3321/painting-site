## Where to send it

Elements is produced by **BangOn!NYC**. Vendor and art-program applications usually open through:

1. **elementsfestival.com** — check the "Vendors" or "Get Involved" section (often opens late winter / early spring).
2. **BangOn!NYC** site at **bangonnyc.com** — they list contact emails for their festivals there.
3. DM **@elements_fest** and **@bangonnyc** on Instagram asking when 2026 artist/vendor apps open and the best email to send to in the meantime.
4. Try **info@bangonnyc.com** or **art@bangonnyc.com** as a starting point.
5. Get on the Elements newsletter so you catch the official application window the day it drops.

---

## One last thing

Elements is typically end of August / Labor Day-ish weekend, so artist apps usually open late winter or early spring. Sending now is *good* — early applications get remembered, and the art program at Elements is curated, not first-come-first-served.

The strongest angle here is the elemental imagery in your existing catalog. Lean into it. *Pulse of Green*, *Inner Fire*, *Coral Hour*, *Twilight*, *Golden Hour* — half your titles already speak this festival's language without you having to translate.
