# Festival Outreach — Pitch Template

Customize the bracketed parts per festival. Keep it under 300 words — coordinators skim.

---

## Subject line options

Pick whichever fits the vibe:

- Live oil painter + vendor application — [Festival Name] [Year]
- Artist application: live portrait painting at [Festival Name]
- Live painting + art vending — Abby Starmer Art
- Vendor / artist application — oil portraits, prints, stickers

---

## Main email template

> **Hi [Name / Vendor Team / Art Coordinator],**
>
> My name is Abby Starmer, and I'd love to be considered as a live painter and/or art vendor for [Festival Name] [Year].
>
> I'm an oil painter based in Nashville. My work focuses on **faces and eyes** — portraiture done in oil, in a style you don't usually see in the festival circuit (most live painters lean abstract or cosmic; I lean intimate and human). At festivals I offer two things, depending on what works for your programming:
>
> **1. Live painting / activation.** I set up an easel and paint a real-time portrait throughout the weekend. People can sit briefly, watch the process, and the finished piece becomes part of the festival's art collection or a giveaway. It's a slower, more meditative draw than most live art setups — people stop, sit down, and stay.
>
> **2. Vendor booth.** Original oil paintings, archival fine art prints (paper + canvas), and weatherproof vinyl stickers. Price points from $8 stickers up through originals, so there's something for every festival-goer.
>
> I'm drawn to [Festival Name] specifically because [one sentence — their art program, their vibe, an artist you love who played last year, the way they curate vendors, etc.]. I'd love to bring my work into that space.
>
> You can see the body of work here: **abbystarmerart.org** · Instagram **@abbystarmerart**
>
> Happy to send a full vendor packet, references from past events, or jump on a quick call. I appreciate you taking a look.
>
> Warmly,
> Abby Starmer
> abbystarmerart@gmail.com
> [Your phone number]
> abbystarmerart.org · @abbystarmerart

---

## What to attach to the email

- **Portfolio:** link to abbystarmerart.org (no PDF needed — your site is good enough)
- **1–2 photos:** ideally one of you painting in action, one of a finished piece you're proud of
- **Optional one-pager:** quick PDF with your bio, what you offer, price ranges, booth needs (power, 10×10 canopy space, table). Save this once, send forever.

---

## Customizing per festival — the one sentence that matters

That `[one sentence about why this festival]` line is what separates you from form-letter applicants. A few examples by festival type:

- **Electric Forest:** "Electric Forest's commitment to its art program — and the way Sherwood Forest becomes a gallery — is exactly the kind of immersive space I want my work to live in."
- **Imagine Music Festival:** "Imagine's art village in Atlanta has been on my radar for years, and your curation of live painters has shaped a lot of what I've watched in this scene."
- **Lost Lands:** "Lost Lands has built one of the most art-forward bass festivals in the country, and I'd love to bring an oil portrait setup into that environment."
- **Bonnaroo:** "I'm a Nashville-based artist, and Bonnaroo is hometown to me — Where in the Woods and Centeroo are spaces I've dreamed about being part of for years."
- **Hulaween / Okeechobee:** "The blend of music, nature, and visual art at [Festival] is the closest thing I've seen to what I want my work to be in conversation with."

---

## Follow-up cadence

- If you don't hear back in **2 weeks**: send a short bump.
  > "Hi [Name], following up on my note from [date] — totally understand you're swamped. Just wanted to make sure my application reached the right inbox. Thanks!"
- If still nothing in **another 2 weeks**: try a different contact at the org (Instagram DM the festival's main account, ask who handles vendors).
- After **6 weeks of silence**: move on, but keep them on your list for next year.
