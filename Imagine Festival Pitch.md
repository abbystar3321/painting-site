## What to attach to the email

- One photo of you painting (even an at-home easel shot works — they want to see the activation visually)
- One photo of a finished portrait you love (Whisper, Iridescent, or Soft Spoken would all be strong choices)
- That's it — your site does the rest of the work

---

## Where to send it

Imagine vendor and artist applications usually open on **imaginefestival.com/vendors** (or under the "Get Involved" tab). If applications aren't open yet, the move is:

1. DM @imaginefest on Instagram asking when artist/vendor apps open for 2026, and the best email to send to in the meantime.
2. Also try **info@iris-presents.com** (Iris Presents produces Imagine) or **vendors@imaginefestival.com**.
3. Get on their newsletter at imaginefestival.com so you catch the official application window the day it drops.

---

## One last thing

Imagine is Labor Day weekend, so artist apps typically open winter/early spring. Sending now is *good* — they remember names that come in early.
